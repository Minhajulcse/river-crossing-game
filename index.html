Rules

How to Play

Every action is checked by the puzzle engine.

1 · Select, then act

Click or tap characters to select them. The game prevents invalid capacity or side selections. Confirm a crossing only when you are happy with the state.

2 · Think about the state, not just the move

After every crossing, both banks are validated. Dangerous combinations can fail even when the boat movement itself is legal.

3 · Hints are progressive

Each level gives three hints. The first is broad, the second is more specific, and the third is intentionally close to the solution without automatically playing it for you.

4 · Score for efficiency

Moves, time, mistakes, hints, and remaining resources all affect the star rating. Best performance is saved locally in your browser.

5 · Controls

R restarts the current level. Esc opens the menu. On supported levels, you can also use keyboard focus and Enter.
